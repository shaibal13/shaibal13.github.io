About

About me

My research mottoMake AI smaller, faster, and useful, so it runs where people need it.

I am a PhD candidate in the Computer Science and Engineering Department at Oakland University, and a graduate research assistant at the Edge Intelligent System Lab under the supervision of Dr. Lanyu Xu.

Before beginning my PhD, I gained nearly four years of experience as a software developer at Code Aristos, Bangladesh. I hold a Master's in Computer Science from Oakland University and a Bachelor's in Computer Science and Engineering from North South University, Bangladesh.

Beyond the lab, I love to explore new places and read history and suspense. Read my travel stories and see my bookshelf on Beyond the Lab.

Research interests

  • LLM systems
  • Edge deployment & inference
  • Connected health
  • Autonomous driving perception

I am actively seeking an internship or co-op in Efficient AI, Machine Learning, and LLM Systems. Please download my CV and feel free to reach out via email at shaibalsaha@oakland.edu to discuss potential collaborations or roles.
